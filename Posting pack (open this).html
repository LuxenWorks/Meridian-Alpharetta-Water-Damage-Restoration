Ask the contractor which license fits, then confirm it with the state. Q: What does a sagging ceiling mean? A: A sagging ceiling has taken on water, the American Red Cross explains, which makes it heavy and dangerous. Nobody stands under it, pets leave too, and 911 is the call if it starts to come down. Q: How soon does wet material need to dry? A: EPA's mold guide wants water-damaged areas and items dry in 24 to 48 hours, and its flood page names wood, drywall, carpet and furniture as the materials that can grow mold after more than 24 hours wet. If a pipe does freeze, Gwinnett's advice is to turn the water off at the main valve and let the pipes thaw. Photograph any damp wall or floor before anything is moved.
