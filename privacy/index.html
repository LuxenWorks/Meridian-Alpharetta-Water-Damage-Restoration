A plain letter about your details

Hello, and thanks for reading. This letter explains, in the order a visitor would meet them, what happens to details connected with a visit to meridianrestorationga.com or a call to the number shown there. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

What the pages collect

The web host keeps ordinary server records: the visitor's IP address, the type of browser and device, the pages requested and the time. The pages collect nothing typed in by a visitor, so a name, address or phone number reaches Meridian Alpharetta Water Damage Restoration only when a caller chooses to say it aloud. Visits and taps on the phone number may be counted so that the business can see which pages lead to calls.

What a call involves

Calls to the number on the pages go through a call-tracking service. It notes the caller's phone number, the time and length of the call, and which page or source the call came from. Calls may be recorded; when one is, a short notice plays as the call begins.

Who sees call details

Details given on a call, such as a name, an address and a description of the problem, are passed to the local contractor who takes the call, so that a visit can be arranged. The call-tracking service handles them on the way. They are not posted anywhere public.

Cookies and browser settings

The pages may set cookies or similar tools to measure visits and the source of a call. A visitor who prefers less tracking can block cookies, or switch on a browser's Global Privacy Control setting, and the pages still read and work the same way.

Asking about a call

A caller who wants to know what is held about a call, or wants it removed, can phone the number shown on the pages and ask. The request is passed to whoever keeps the call records.

Children

The pages are written for adults who own or manage property. Details from children are not sought.

Changes to this letter

When the wording changes, the new letter replaces the old one at /privacy/ and carries a new date.

Reaching the business about privacy
