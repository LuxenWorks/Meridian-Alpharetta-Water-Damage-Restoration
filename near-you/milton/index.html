Near Alpharetta

Water damage in Milton's newer houses

Milton City Hall stands at 2006 Heritage Walk, and the houses around the city are young by Georgia standards. The Census Bureau's 2024 five-year estimates date the median Milton home to 2002, plus or minus 2 years. Of the city's 15,594 homes, 58.5 percent date from 2000 onward and 9.8 percent from before 1990. Newer does not mean immune: UGA's maintenance checklist asks owners to test the water-heater relief valve, check washer hoses and look for roof leaks in any house. Which Milton desk answers what

Milton's Community Development department handles building permits, and its residential page sorts the common jobs. Permits are required for moving or adding plumbing, for new or replacement HVAC equipment and for finishing a basement, attic or bonus room. They are generally not required for painting, floor coverings, cabinets or counter tops. Drywall and repair after water damage appear nowhere on the page, so that question belongs to the permit desk before a wall is opened.

City Hall is at 2006 Heritage Walk, Milton, GA 30004, phone 678-242-2500, Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Once an application is accepted, the city's residential page says approval or revision comments follow within 10 business days. Georgia's contractor license rules are on the hiring page.

Rain on record

Helene's rain, and two checks before the next one

Hurricane Helene's rain in 2024 averaged 9 to 11 inches across Fulton County, according to the National Weather Service's radar-based county report, and the county saw two flash flood emergencies. Radar describes a county, not a street, so your own yard may have seen more or less. UGA's checklist adds two checks worth making before any long rain: look for roof and attic leaks, and confirm that gutters and downspouts discharge water 2 to 3 feet from the house.

Asked and answered

Asked in Milton

Do drywall and floor repairs need a Milton permit?

Milton's residential page lists moving or adding plumbing fixtures, HVAC replacement and finishing a basement as permit jobs, and painting, floor coverings, cabinets and counter tops as generally exempt. Drywall and water damage repair do not appear, so City Hall, 678-242-2500, can settle it before walls are opened.

Who looks after yard drainage in Milton?

Public Works runs Milton's stormwater program, the city's stormwater page explains, and Community Development takes plan reviews and site inspections. Fulton County's water page names Milton among the places the county serves with water and sewer, so a fault in the main line goes to the county.

Who can call from Milton?

Anyone with water inside a Milton house: an owner, or a property manager acting for one. Of the occupied Milton homes, about 27.5 percent are rented, 4,181 of 15,225, and a renter begins with the landlord.
