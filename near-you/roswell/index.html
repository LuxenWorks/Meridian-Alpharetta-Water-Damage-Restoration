Near Alpharetta

Water damage in Roswell: 1980s houses, two water systems

Roswell City Hall, at 38 Hill Street, is where permit questions land, and the Census Bureau's 2024 five-year estimates describe a city built mostly in the 1980s and 1990s. The median home went up in 1989, give or take 2 years. Of the 37,439 homes, 32.8 percent date from the 1980s, 20.5 percent from the 1990s and 54.8 percent from before 1990. Water service is split too, between the city and Fulton County. When a water heater or washer is the source, read about appliance and water heater leaks, then call (678) 929-5373 and give the address and the wet rooms.

The Roswell HUB, and who answers its phone

Roswell runs building and other permits through its online Permitting & Licensing HUB. City Hall, at 38 Hill Street, Roswell, GA 30075, answers questions at (770) 641-3727 on weekdays between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. The city's permitting page says Georgia law requires contractors who hold a license to get the needed building permits before they begin work, and it suggests that property owners confirm whether a permit is needed before work starts. Water damage repair is not mentioned.

An owner who hires a contractor generally does not need a HUB account, since the contractor applies under their own name and license type. Georgia's rules on who needs that license are on the hiring page.

Rain on record

A very wet 2024 on the gauge

The Atlanta weather station logged 63.18 inches of rain in 2024, where a normal year brings 49.71, with 4.32 inches on September 26 alone, and the National Weather Service ranks it the 14th wettest year in that station's record. Radar put Hurricane Helene's rain across Fulton County, which includes Roswell, at 9 to 11 inches on average. Those are station and county figures, so rain at your address may differ.

Asked and answered

Roswell, in short answers

Which water system serves a Roswell house?

The city's water page says its plant serves only part of Roswell, about 5,600 customers, and that Fulton County serves everyone else and handles all sewer lines. For leaks, Roswell lists (770) 641-3707 on weekdays; after hours the number is (770) 640-4100. Fulton County Water takes (770) 640-3040. Your water bill names the provider.

Does hiring a contractor in Roswell mean making an account?

The city's permitting page says an owner who hires a contractor generally does not need a HUB account, since the contractor applies under their own name and license type. An owner doing the work personally does. Water damage repair is not mentioned, so ask City Hall at (770) 641-3727.

Who can call from Roswell?

Anyone with water inside a Roswell house. Roughly 28.1 percent of occupied homes are rented, 9,990 of 35,540, and a renter begins with the landlord or property manager. Note the address, the source, the wet rooms and roughly how old the house is, then call (678) 929-5373.
