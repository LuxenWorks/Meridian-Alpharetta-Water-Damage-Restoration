Water damage in Johns Creek: 1990s houses and creeks

Johns Creek City Hall, at 11360 Lakefield Drive, sits in a city that mostly grew up in the 1990s. By the Census Bureau's 2024 five-year estimates the median home dates to 1995, plus or minus 1 year, and 45.1 percent of the city's 29,895 homes went up in that decade with another 27.1 percent earlier. Thirty years on, those houses have aging water heaters, washer hoses and roofs, all three on UGA's checklist. If the water came out of a wall or a floor, read about burst pipe water damage, then dial (678) 929-5373 once you know which room is wet.

What the Building Division asks of a contractor

Johns Creek's Building Division lists 678-512-3250 on its permit guidelines. City Hall, 11360 Lakefield Drive, Johns Creek, GA 30097, answers on 678-512-3200 between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. on weekdays. The city's building FAQs say a permit is valid for 180 days and that some permits, among them water heater change-outs, service meter upgrades, reroofs and siding, can be issued over the counter. The city's pages do not say which repairs after water damage need one, so that call goes to the Building Division.

A contractor applying for permits shows the city a copy of the business license, the building FAQs say, along with a State License where state law calls for one. Which jobs state law covers is on the hiring page.

Rain on record

Floodplain, creeks and two wet years

About 6 percent of Johns Creek lies inside a regulated floodplain, the city says, and owners are asked to contact Community Development before altering property there. The same floodplain page cautions that flood damage falls outside a typical homeowners policy, so your own policy is the document to read. For scale, the Atlanta weather station recorded 53.81 inches of rain in 2021 against a normal of 49.71, and 66.99 inches in 2020. A gauge in Atlanta cannot say how much fell on your street.

Asked and answered

Johns Creek, asked and answered

Who takes a water or sewer report in Johns Creek?

Johns Creek's public works FAQs list 770-640-3040 for water problems and 404-612-3061 for sewer problems, with press 1 for sewer emergencies after hours. For flooding on a road, the number is City Hall's, 678-512-3200. Those offices cover the street side; the contractor starts with the wet rooms.

Does a homeowners policy cover flooding here?

Johns Creek's floodplain page states that flood damage falls outside a typical homeowners policy, and it directs owners to the National Flood Insurance Program, in which the city participates. What your own policy pays is for your insurer to say. Georgia's insurance office advises photographing damage and keeping receipts.

Who can call from Johns Creek?

Homeowners, landlords and property managers with water inside a Johns Creek house. About 19.6 percent of occupied homes are rented, 5,680 of 28,948, and a renter begins with the landlord. Have the street address, what caused it, which rooms are wet and how old the house is, then call (678) 929-5373.
