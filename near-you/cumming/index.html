Near Alpharetta

Water damage in Cumming, where about half the homes are rented

Cumming City Hall, at 100 Main Street, is the hub for the city's permits and inspections, and the Census Bureau's 2024 five-year estimates count about 2,879 homes inside the city, give or take 267. The median home went up in 1998, give or take 5 years, with about 1,360 built in 2000 or later and about 1,020 before 1990. Estimates this small carry wide margins, so read them as a rough picture. Carpet soaked through belongs with water extraction and drying, and a Cumming address with wet rooms is a reason to call (678) 929-5373 and say which ones.

City desk or county desk

Inside the city, the Planning & Zoning department at City Hall, 100 Main Street, Cumming, GA 30040, handles building permits and inspections. Its page says the department reviews development plans, issues land disturbance and construction permits and inspects trade work in all phases. The department's number is 770-781-2024, and City Hall's main line is 770-781-2010, open between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. on weekdays. Inspection requests go in by phone, on weekdays from 8:30 to 4:30, and voicemail requests are not accepted. Neither of the department's pages mentions water damage repair.

Forsyth County runs its own building office, and the county's pages list its phone number two different ways, so ask whether the city's desk or the county's covers your address. Georgia's contractor license rules are on the hiring page, which lists both county numbers.

Rain on record

Big Creek, Dick Creek and a wet 2024

For Hurricane Helene in 2024, the National Weather Service's county report puts radar-estimated rain in Forsyth County at 7 to 9 inches on average, with Big Creek and Dick Creek gauges reaching Minor Flood stage and impacts to the Big Creek Greenway and low parts of Cox Circle. The Atlanta station logged 63.18 inches for the year against a normal of 49.71. Neither figure measures your yard.

Asked and answered

What Cumming callers want to know

Who answers a water or sewer emergency in Cumming?

Forsyth County Water and Sewer lists 770-781-2160 for after-hours, weekend and holiday emergencies, and its pages do not say whether the number covers customers of the City of Cumming. The city's website links Cumming Utilities but lists no emergency number. Your water bill names the provider.

How does Cumming take an inspection request?

Planning & Zoning says to call during business hours; voicemail requests are not accepted. A request carries the permit number, project name, address, caller's name and phone number, and inspection type, and it goes in no later than the day before the inspection is wanted. Requests are taken on weekdays from 8:30 to 4:30.

Who can call from Cumming?

Anyone with water inside a Cumming house, owner or manager. The Census estimate has about 52 percent of occupied homes in the city rented, with a wide margin of error, so many callers begin with a landlord. Gather the address, what caused it, which rooms are wet and roughly how old the house is, then call (678) 929-5373.
